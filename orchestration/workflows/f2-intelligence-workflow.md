# F2 Intelligence Workflow

1. PROJECT INTAKE: Coordinator receives requirement.
2. REQUIREMENTS DISCOVERY: AG-004 produces requirements.
3. ARCHITECTURE DESIGN: AG-005 designs architecture.
4. DATABASE DESIGN: AG-007 designs data model.
5. UX/UI DESIGN: AG-006 designs experience and screens.
6. CROSS-REVIEW: Specialists review related deliverables.
7. CONSISTENCY VALIDATION: Coordinator integrates and validates.
8. QUALITY AUDIT: AG-002 audits independently.
9. REMEDIATION: Defects return to responsible agent.
10. FINAL PACKAGE: Coordinator assembles analysis package.
11. HUMAN APPROVAL: Request approval before F3.
